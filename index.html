01 — About

Building, learning,
and solving.

I'm a first-year Electrical Engineering Co-op student at Concordia University in Montreal, currently serving as Director of Hardware for Space Concordia's Avionics division and contributing to the Robotics division as well.

My work spans PCB design, embedded firmware, and hardware bring-up and testing — bare-metal STM32 development, CAN bus communication, and RF link design for rover subsystems. I hold an Amateur Radio Operator certification and I'm trilingual in English, French, and Portuguese.
